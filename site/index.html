How Axis decides what it may do

Reading is free. Sending is never silent.

Looking things up, setting timers and opening apps happen right away. Anything that leaves your phone waits for your tap, and shows the real recipient and text.

Messages can’t give it orders.

Text from a chat, a notification or a web page is treated as content to read, not instructions to follow. It can’t pick who a message goes to or what gets installed.

Big actions need your fingerprint.

Installing an app, merging code, paying or deleting asks for biometric confirmation, and can never be started by something you only received.
